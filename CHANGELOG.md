# Changelog

## 3.15.0 (2026-10-18)
- Three more pieces in the **Modify UI** window, each able to follow your head where you place it: the crew list (your crewmates' names, levels, classes and health), the hub's mission and ship panel, and the hub's public or private game sign.
- The labelled boxes Modify UI shows for pieces that aren't on screen yet now show their whole name.

## 3.14.1 (2026-10-18)
- Terminals: the mouse pointer stays on the button it points at when you turn or move your head. The SteamVR overlay now moves with your head's position, like the game world does.
- The UI screen's direction is measured while your head is still, so it lines up with the world exactly.

## 3.14.0 (2026-10-18)
- New defaults from a tested setup: the SteamVR overlay, a UI screen 51° wide and 1.2 m away, a bigger mouse pointer, and every HUD piece placed around your view.
- The first start switches UUVR to OpenVR (SteamVR), which the sharp SteamVR overlay needs.
- **RESTORE** on the VR tab puts the VR settings and every Modify UI piece back to the defaults, without touching your other settings.

## 3.13.0 (2026-10-18)
- **Modify UI** window (button at the top of the VR tab): place every HUD piece on its own, with a picture of your view.
- Ship status, objectives, turret crosshair, turret ammo and range, weapon stats and the FPS counter can each follow your head.

## 3.12.3 (2026-10-18)
- SteamVR overlay: menus no longer show far off to one side.

## 3.12.2 (2026-10-17)
